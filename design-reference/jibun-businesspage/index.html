FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need an app?

No. It opens in the phone's browser.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, on modern phones. The QR code is the backup.

What if the other phone has no NFC?

They scan the QR code.

Can I change my details later?

Yes. You get revisions and small edits; more later are paid per request.

What happens after 4 years?

Renew for a small fee, or upgrade. After a 30-day grace period a lapsed page shows a "please contact us" screen.

Can I upgrade Starter to Standard later?

Yes. You keep your link and card and only pay the difference.

What if I lose my card?

We can disable it and give you a new one.

Is my info safe?

You choose what appears on your page. Taps are counted without storing anyone's IP address.
